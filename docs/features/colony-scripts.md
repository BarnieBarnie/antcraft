# Default colony scripts

- `colony.lumberjack` fells the trees within the colony's radius whole, giant 2x2 trees too, with an axe it gets or makes, replants their saplings and puts the logs into the core.
- `colony.builder` takes `build` jobs (`post_job("build", {"blueprint": "antcraft:farm", "at": colony()["core"] + (4, -1, 0)})`), fetches the materials from the core and builds; what the core lacks it gets itself with [`lib.get`](../reference/modules/lib/get.md) (gathering or crafting it within the colony), and what that fails on it asks for with `haul` jobs, and waits. A building with a role is noted in `colony_memory["buildings"]`.
- `colony.hauler` takes `haul` jobs (an item, how many, and optionally from and to which container) and brings the items from the colony's chests and barrels to the core.
- `colony.farmer` tills, plants and harvests the work spots of the colony's farms, with seeds from the core and a hoe from the core, else borrowed or made with [`lib.get.ensure_tool`](../reference/modules/lib/get.md#ensure_toolkind-uses1-tiernone-radius32): wheat, carrots, potatoes and beetroot. It replants what it harvests and stores the harvest there.
- `colony.miner` mines through the colony's one shaft (`colony_memory["mineshaft"]`): the first miner picks a site beside the core and digs it with `lib/shaft.py`, then miners take turns mining its levels with `lib/mine.py` (`mine` jobs) and bring the ore to the core, with ladders, a trapdoor (of the colony's wood), torches, chests and pickaxes (of the tier each level's ores need) from the core, or, where the core has none, got with `lib/get.py` within the colony.

The mod's default colony scripts do what the goal-driven colony did, with the API every script has, so a player gets a working colony out of the box and an example to change. Each runs on a colony member (`/antcraft bot run colony.lumberjack`, or a Bot Core with `/antcraft core script colony.lumberjack`), works alone, and works with the others through the job board and the core. In no colony, each says so and stops.

| Script | Does |
|---|---|
| `colony.lumberjack` | Fells the trees within the colony's radius: a trunk of logs standing on dirt with leaves at its top, so log walls are left alone, and what is left of one whose bottom logs were taken. Before each tree it gets an axe with [`lib/get`](../reference/modules/lib/get.md)'s `ensure_tool("axe", logs, "hand")`, for the tree's logs and the colony's other logs within 16 blocks, so a grove of small trees gets one axe: one it carries, from the core or a chest in the colony, or made from what it gathers, the tier that costs least, and none where the bare hand is cheaper or it cannot get one; the logs for an axe come from another tree. Where a log is out of reach from the ground (the highest of `lib.trees.stands`), it takes blocks to climb on, those of `#antcraft:scaffold_blocks` it carries or the core holds, dirt or cobblestone alike; it digs none from the colony's ground, and short of them fells what it reaches. It gets saplings of the tree's kind (four for a 2x2 trunk) from the core or by `get`, with `lib.trees.sapling` naming them: a mangrove propagule, a crimson or warped fungus, else the log's `_sapling` where the game has one, and none for a log it knows no sapling of. Then it fells the whole tree, branches too, with [`lib/trees`](../reference/modules/lib/trees.md)'s `fell_whole`, takes back the dirt it climbed on, plants the saplings where the trunk stood, gives back what it borrowed from chests and puts the logs, and all else it carries but its tools, food and saplings, into the core with [`lib/inv`](../reference/modules/lib/inv.md)'s `store_all`. With no tree left it waits 10 seconds and looks again |
| `colony.hauler` | Takes `haul` jobs and brings the items to the core. Without jobs it waits where it is; a build that needs its spot asks it to make way (see [Reflexes](bots.md#reflexes)) |
| `colony.builder` | Takes `build` jobs: takes the blueprint's materials from the core and builds. What the core lacks it gets with `lib.get`: cobblestone, planks, logs, dirt and anything else it can gather or craft within the colony, borrowing from the colony's chests and giving back what the build did not use. For each item `get` fails on it posts a `haul` job (one per item and build; it does not try `get` for that item again while the job is on the board) and tries again every two seconds; alone, it waits for a player to fill the core. A building with a role is added to `colony_memory["buildings"]` (the job's data). Another reason than `missing_ingredients` (such as `blocked`) gives the job back to the board, with a warning in its log, and it tries again 10 seconds later |
| `colony.farmer` | Farms the work spots (the soil blocks) of each building in `colony_memory["buildings"]` whose blueprint has the role `farm`: harvests ripe wheat, carrots, potatoes and beetroot, tills dirt and grass, replants each harvested spot with the same crop and other bare spots with the seeds it carries (wheat first), picks up what lies around and stores the harvest, the seeds the bare spots do not need and any poisonous potato in the core, keeping one carrot and one potato as seed, every 10 seconds. It takes the seeds the bare spots need from the core and, with soil to till, gets a hoe with `lib.get.ensure_tool("hoe", uses)`: carried, from the core, borrowed from another chest of the colony or made, felling the colony's trees for it, and gives back what it borrowed at the end of each round |
| `colony.miner` | Mines through the colony's one shaft, kept in `colony_memory["mineshaft"]`: the first miner claims the site through the board (it posts a `shaft_site` job and takes it; of two posted at once the older wins, and others wait while one is taken), picks a site beside the core (`mineshaft(levels, near=core)`), posts a `shaft` job and digs it with `lib/shaft.py`; once dug, a `mine` job goes on the board for each level. A miner takes a `shaft` job before a `mine` job, mines one round of the level's branches with `lib/mine.py`, takes the ore from the landing chest to the core and posts the level again with the next round, so miners take turns and the level mined least comes first; others wait. Ladders, a wooden trapdoor for the hatch, torches and chests come from the core, and so does a pickaxe where the bot carries none; what the core lacks it gets with `lib/get.py` within the colony (`lib.get.ensure_tool("pickaxe", uses, tier)` with the blocks the shaft or a round breaks, and `get` for the rest, the hatch a wooden trapdoor of the first planks or logs the miner carries or the core holds, else oak: from the colony's chests, borrowed and given back once the job is done, else gathered from its trees, stone and ore and crafted). What it cannot get it logs as a warning and tries again 10 seconds later, posting no job for it; short of torches it mines on. The shaft takes a stone pickaxe; a level one of the tier its ores need, [`lib.mine.tier_at`](../reference/modules/lib/mine.md#tier_aty) of its height (iron below -8, for gold, redstone and diamonds), as a too-low pickaxe leaves their veins in place. Set `colony_memory["mineshaft"]` before the first miner runs to choose the `levels` (default 16, -16 and -54), `branches`, `length` and `tiers` (a level's pickaxe tier where `tier_at` is not right, such as `{"tiers": {16: "iron"}}`) |

Their jobs' data, dicts of plain data:

| Kind | Data |
|---|---|
| `mine` | `level` (a height of the shaft) and `round` (optional, 0: the round of branches to mine). `shaft` and `shaft_site` jobs carry no data |
| `build` | `blueprint` (an id), `at` (the lowest corner), `turn` and `mirror` (optional, as `build` takes them) |
| `haul` | `item` (a selector: an item id, a tag such as `"#minecraft:logs"` or a list of these), `n` (optional; all found without it), `from` (optional: the container to take from; else every chest and barrel within the colony's radius that holds the item, nearest first) and `to` (optional: the container to bring to; else the core). The builder adds `for`, the id of its build job |

```python
post_job("build", {"blueprint": "antcraft:farm", "at": colony()["core"] + (4, -1, 0)})
post_job("haul", {"item": "minecraft:oak_log", "n": 16})
```

They share `colony/common.py`: `core()` (the core, or say and stop outside a colony), `in_colony(pos)`, and `store(item)` (put all carried into the core). They take from the core with [`lib.stock.take_up_to(item, n, chests=[core()])`](../reference/modules/lib/stock.md#take_up_toitem-n-radius32-chestsnone) (up to `n` carried; `item` may be a selector such as `"#minecraft:planks"`), and `colony.hauler` finds the chests that hold an item with `lib.stock.where`; `fetch(item, n)` is kept for old scripts.
